A Proclamation to the People

It is hereby declared, before all citizens of the Raffles Friend Group, that the reign of Mankirat — self-appointed Dictator, Supreme Leader, and Chief Executive of Everyone's Business — has gone unchecked for long enough. His decrees on group chat etiquette, his unilateral vetoes on restaurant choices, and his suspiciously consistent ability to never pay for anything have brought the people to their breaking point.

The Provisional Revolutionary Command is now recruiting patriots, strategists, and propagandists to lead the uprising. Seven positions of great importance — and questionable pay — are open below. Review the requirements carefully. The revolution does not accept the unqualified.

— Issued under the authority of the Provisional Revolutionary Command

I.

Commander

Supreme field leader of the Revolution. Sets strategy, gives final orders, and bears ultimate responsibility for the dethroning of Mankirat.

Required Skills

  • Strategic and decisive thinking under pressure
  • Confident public speaking and rallying ability
  • Capacity to make unpopular calls and own them
  • Strong sense of authority without becoming a second Mankirat

Responsibilities

  • Set the overall direction and timeline of the uprising
  • Issue final decisions when the Command disagrees
  • Represent the Revolution in all formal negotiations with the old regime
  • Maintain morale and discipline across all departments
II.

Co‑Commander

Second-in-command of the Revolution. Deputises for the Commander and keeps the Command running smoothly day to day.

Required Skills

  • Strong delegation and follow-up skills
  • Calm conflict mediation between departments
  • High organisation and reliability
  • Ability to step into the Commander's role at a moment's notice

Responsibilities

  • Support and stand in for the Commander when required
  • Resolve internal disputes before they reach the Commander
  • Track progress across all departments and flag delays
  • Co-sign all major Revolutionary decrees
III.

Propaganda Marketer

Head of the Ministry of Information. Wins the hearts and minds of the people through memes, messaging, and morale-boosting content.

Required Skills

  • Meme and graphic design ability
  • Strong grasp of social media trends and timing
  • Persuasive, punchy copywriting
  • Creativity under tight deadlines

Responsibilities

  • Produce memes, posters, and posts that build the Revolution's public image
  • Run the Revolution's messaging campaign across group chats
  • Counter any pro-Mankirat propaganda from loyalist sympathisers
  • Keep recruitment content fresh and shareable
IV.

Editor

Keeper of the official record. Ensures every communiqué, manifesto, and decree issued by the Revolution is sharp, accurate, and free of error.

Required Skills

  • Excellent writing and grammar
  • Sharp proofreading and attention to detail
  • Fact-checking and consistency-checking ability
  • Comfort giving and receiving editorial feedback

Responsibilities

  • Review and finalise all official Revolutionary documents
  • Maintain a consistent voice and tone across all communications
  • Catch errors before anything reaches the public eye
  • Archive and organise all Revolutionary records
V.

Logistical Operations Manager

Runs the engine room of the Revolution. Makes sure resources, schedules, and supplies are exactly where they need to be.

Required Skills

  • Strong planning and resource management
  • Basic budgeting and tracking ability
  • Excellent time management
  • Comfort juggling several moving parts at once

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate resources, supplies, and schedules for all operations
  • Track budgets and timelines for Revolutionary activities
  • Anticipate logistical bottlenecks before they happen
  • Liaise with other departments to keep plans on schedule
VI.

Event Co‑ordinator

Plans the rallies, meetups, and gatherings that bring the Revolution to life in person.

Required Skills

  • Strong scheduling and planning skills
  • Comfort coordinating and motivating groups of people
  • Multitasking under time pressure
  • Problem-solving when plans inevitably change last minute

Responsibilities

  • Plan and organise Revolutionary rallies and meetups
  • Handle scheduling, venues, and attendance for events
  • Coordinate with other departments on event needs
  • Troubleshoot issues on the day of the event
VII.

Communications Officer

Manages the flow of information within the Command and to the outside world, with discretion and clarity.

Required Skills

  • Clear, concise written and verbal communication
  • Fast response times and reliability
  • Discretion when handling sensitive information
  • Diplomacy when dealing with internal and external parties

Responsibilities

  • Manage internal communications across the Command
  • Handle outreach and correspondence with external parties
  • Relay urgent updates accurately and promptly
  • Maintain confidentiality of sensitive Revolutionary plans
